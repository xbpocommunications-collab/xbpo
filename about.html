03 / THE FLOOR MODEL

Different roles. One campaign flow.

A final expense calling floor works best when the opening conversation, qualification, handoff, verification and review are treated as connected stages rather than isolated tasks.

01

Fronters open the conversation

Fronters work the first contact, follow the campaign opener, qualify the prospect and move valid conversations to the required next stage.

02

Closers carry the sales conversation

Closers receive qualified opportunities and handle the deeper campaign conversation using the approved process and campaign requirements.

03

Verification and QA protect the process

Verification and quality review help keep records, call handling and campaign outcomes consistent with the operating brief.
