01 / ROLE OWNERSHIP

What this role owns on the floor.

The closer receives qualified conversations and takes ownership of the deeper sales-stage discussion. The role requires stronger objection handling, conversation control and disciplined use of the campaign process.

01Receive qualified handoffs from fronters with minimal loss of context.
02Lead the deeper campaign conversation and handle prospect questions.
03Use the approved sales process and required campaign checkpoints.
04Document the final outcome accurately and route any required verification.
05Coordinate with verifiers, QA and team leads when a case needs review.
02 / DAILY RHYTHM

How the work connects to the campaign.

The exact script and criteria vary by campaign, but the role still sits inside a clear operating flow from brief to live handling, handoff and review.

01Brief

Understand the campaign objective, call flow and required outcomes.

02Live floor

Execute the assigned role while keeping call notes and dispositions clean.

03Handoff

Move the prospect or record to the correct next stage with context.

04Review

Use feedback, coaching and quality observations to improve handling.
