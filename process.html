02 / FLOOR HANDOFFS

The handoff between roles matters as much as the call itself.

For final expense workflows, the first conversation, closer handoff, verification and review need clear ownership so the prospect does not get lost between stages.

F

Fronter

Starts the conversation and establishes qualification.

C

Closer

Handles the deeper sales-stage conversation after a valid handoff.

V

Verifier

Confirms campaign-required information and process checkpoints.

Q

QA

Reviews handling quality and feeds observations back to the floor.

03 / DAILY CONTROL

Campaign rhythm continues after launch.

A campaign is not a one-time setup. Call outcomes, agent questions, quality observations and handoff issues create the information needed to refine execution.

Opening reviewBrief, targets and role allocation.
Live-floor supportQuestions, escalations and handoff clarity.
Call reviewSelected conversations and disposition quality.
Coaching loopFeedback returned to agents and team leads.
04 / WHAT CLIENTS DEFINE

The campaign owner sets the rules that matter.

Audience

Target profile, geography, timing and campaign exclusions.

Conversation

Approved wording, qualifying questions and required disclosures.

Outcome

What counts as a valid transfer, appointment, lead or completed interaction.
