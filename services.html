03 / ENGAGEMENT DESIGN

Service scope should follow the campaign brief.

Before calling starts

  • Campaign objective and target audience
  • Approved opening and conversation path
  • Qualification, transfer or appointment criteria
  • Required call outcomes and dispositions

During live operations

  • Role ownership across fronters, closers and support
  • Disposition consistency and note quality
  • Escalation path for unclear call outcomes
  • QA observations and coaching feedback
