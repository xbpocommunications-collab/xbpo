01 / ROLE OWNERSHIP

What this role owns on the floor.

The verifier checks campaign-required information after the sales or qualification stage and confirms that the record follows the expected process before it moves forward.

01Review the information passed from the calling stage.
02Conduct the required verification conversation or record check.
03Confirm campaign-defined details and identify missing information.
04Use the correct verification outcome and escalate exceptions.
05Maintain neutral, accurate handling of each verification.
